01

CLIMATE VARIABILITY

Ecosystem Responses to Climate Extremes

I investigate how drought and other climate extremes affect ecosystems, from plant water use and functional traits to large-scale terrestrial carbon cycling.

Selected work:
  • Drought effects on trait space of winter wheat ( New Phytologist, 2024 )
  • Water uptake patterns of pea and barley under drought ( Biogeosciences, 2022 )
  • Contributions to the TRENDY project (Trends and Drivers of Terrestrial Sources and Sinks of Carbon Dioxide, Sitch et al., 2024 ) and terrestrial carbon sink assessments through the Global Carbon Budget ( Friedlingstein et al., 2025 )
02

LAND MANAGEMENT AND ECOSYSTEM RESTORATION

Land Management and Ecosystem Restoration

I study how land management and long-term ecosystem modification influence water, carbon, and greenhouse-gas dynamics, including Swiss peatland carbon dynamics and the assessment of restoration opportunities.

Selected work:
  • Reconstruction of Swiss peatland carbon dynamics (manuscript in preparation)
  • Crop responses to drought under contrasting management systems ( Plant Cell & Environment, 2021 ; New Phytologist, 2024 )
03

MODELLING

Terrestrial Biosphere Modelling

I develop and apply process-based models to investigate interactions among carbon, nitrogen, and greenhouse gas cycling and to connect ecosystem processes across spatial and temporal scales.

My current collaborations also explore links between anthropogenic environmental change and biogeochemical cycling, including the effects of microplastics on marine particle transport.

Selected work:
  • Bayesian data assimilation for carbon-nitrogen modelling ( Preprint )
  • Seasonal cycles of surface N2O fluxes and atmospheric N2O ( Global Biogeochemical Cycles, 2024 )
  • Contributions to global assessments of the carbon , nitrogen , and methane cycles

LOOKING FORWARD

Connecting terrestrial heterogeneity with Earth-system questions

I am interested in improving the representation of terrestrial heterogeneity in process-based models by integrating experiments, observations, and emerging Earth observation products. My goal is to better understand how ecosystem processes shape land-climate interactions and inform climate adaptation, mitigation, and sustainable land management.
